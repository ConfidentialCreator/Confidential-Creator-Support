The Marrow Dispatch is a demo creator on Solana devnet. Its supporters are wallets raised by a script; the transfers and the proofs are real.

Open the creator's page Support with devnet funds

Devnet funds come from a faucet on the support page. No real money moves.

What it does

The fact is public.

A creator's page counts the people who support them this period, and the count can be rebuilt from chain state without the platform. The program — not the index — writes a pledge only when a confidential transfer from the supporter to the creator is in the same transaction.

The amount is sealed.

Every contribution is a Token-2022 confidential transfer. The amount exists only as ciphertext, readable by the supporter, the creator and the holder of the audit key. There is no amount field in the program, the database or the api.

Support recurs, on your terms.

One payment covers 1 to 12 periods of 30 days; the expiry date is shown before you sign. Renewing before the date extends from it, so you never drop out of the count. After expiry there are 3 days of grace. Nothing renews by itself.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect a wallet. One signature derives your confidential keys; they never leave the tab.

  2. 2

    Contribute. The app builds the transactions; your wallet only signs — once for a repeat contribution.

  3. 3

    The program checks the transfer. A pledge without a confidential transfer to the creator in the same transaction is refused.

  4. 4

    The creator decrypts in the browser. The server holds one key, and it only pays for proofs.

Not printed on this page, because it is not true

  • It does not hide who supports. A supporter's wallet is visible on chain by design; anonymity is a different product.

    It does not hide amounts from the audit key. Its holder can read every amount; in the demo that is the operator, keeping the key offline.

  • No selective disclosure and no auditor's tool yet — that is the next release.

    No withdrawal yet: a creator's funds stay in the confidential balance.

    No reminders outside the app — no email.

  • No stablecoin. The token is a demo mint, SUPD, on devnet. No fiat, no KYC.

    The demo does not prove fee economics on mainnet or throughput at thousands of supporters.

Questions

Can anyone see that I support a creator?

Yes. Your wallet and the dates are public; only the amount is sealed.

Who can read my amount?

You, the creator and the holder of the audit key. Not the platform's server.

Does it charge me again by itself?

No. You renew yourself, up to 12 periods at once.

What if the platform goes away?

Every counter can be rebuilt from chain state. The server key only pays for proofs and cannot move tokens.
